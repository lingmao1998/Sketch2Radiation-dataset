# Sketch2Radiation-dataset
Dataset for paper Sketch2Radiation: https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.32311764

Early-stage architectural design often lacks timely feedback on annual facade-level solar radiation, as conventional simulation tools require complete 3D models and time-consuming simulation, slowing design iteration. To address this gap, we propose Sketch2Radiation, a view-aligned surrogate that directly predicts annual solar radiation on visible building surfaces from a single-view digitally generated sketch, conditioned on the corresponding depth (Z-buffer) map, camera pose, and climate descriptor. In contrast to conventional geometry-toperformance surrogates that require complete 3D representations and often produce non-view-aligned outputs, Sketch2Radiation directly maps the designer’s current view to a view-aligned facade radiation map for rapid, in-workflow interpretation and iteration. We release a large-scale (480,000 samples) multi-climate dataset spanning 1000 buildings × 24 views × 20 cities, with annual radiation maps generated from high-fidelity Radiance-based simulation. Experiments in both single-city and multi-city settings show stable and geometryconsistent predictions.

Citation: Mao, Ling, and Changying Xiang. "Sketch2Radiation: Predicting annual solar radiation maps of buildings from architectural sketches with depth and camera pose." Building and Environment (2026): 114725.
